"An oasis of classic porteño elegance seamlessly integrated with contemporary luxury."

Situated on the iconic Avenida Alvear, this distinguished residence embodies the grandeur of Buenos Aires' golden era. The property has been meticulously restored, preserving its original architectural integrity while introducing state-of-the-art comforts.

High ceilings, intricate molding, and original herringbone floors provide a canvas for sophisticated living. The expansive living areas are bathed in natural light, offering sweeping views of the leafy boulevard below, creating a serene sanctuary amidst the vibrant city.

The Spirit of Recoleta

Known for its Parisian-style townhouses, opulent palaces, and luxury boutiques, Recoleta is the undisputed heart of aristocratic Buenos Aires. It is a neighborhood where old-world charm and intellectual soul converge on every tree-lined avenue.
